Clarke Memorial Swim Center

Three outdoor pools in Heather Farm Park — a 50-meter pool open year round, a 25-meter pool with a diving board, and a heated wading pool. 1750 Heather Dr, Walnut Creek.

Status of pool openings and closures

Clarke Swim Center: open for regular programming.

Larkey Swim Center: closed for the season after Labor Day.

The week in the water

Every booking in all three pools, on one calendar. Use the Pools menu to move between the 50-meter pool, the 25-meter pool and the wading pool.

What changed here The published page gives lap swim as a table of lane counts — 8 lanes, then 20, then 8, then 14. It never says what the other lanes are doing, because the city does not run them: the Masters team publishes its own practice times, on its own website. Put both on one calendar and the numbers close exactly — 8 public lanes + 12 Masters lanes = the 20-lane pool, in every morning and midday band. The 8:00–10:30 block, where Masters is out of the water, is the one time all 20 lanes are yours.

What the calendar adds

  • The other twelve lanes. Masters practice appears beside lap swim instead of being invisible.
  • Which water. Lessons are in the wading pool or on 50m Lanes 1–2 — a distinction the table cannot make.
  • Today, not "usually". A four-week lesson session ends on its own date rather than being deleted from a page by hand.
  • Collisions. Two classes booked into one lane show up as a clash, not as a surprise on deck.

Larkey Swim Center — closed for the season

Larkey is a summer pool: a 25×25 meter pool with a one-meter board, plus the all-abilities sprayground. It ran weekends through Labor Day and is now closed.

This week is deliberately empty — the season is over. Page back with the ‹ control to any summer week to see recreational swim, sprayground-only hours and the picnic rentals. · open the full calendar →

An honest limitation, not a feature A closed season currently renders as a blank week. The published page at least says "open weekends only through 9/7" — a sentence someone has to remember to edit. The calendar knows the real answer from its dates and should say "closed for the season, reopens Memorial Day" instead of showing nothing. Logged as an improvement.

Questions for the aquatics office

Everything on this page was transcribed from what Walnut Creek publishes. These are the places where the published sources disagree, go quiet, or where we had to make a call — each one is marked on the calendar entry itself.

  • ConflictEvening lap swim: Monday–Thursday, or Monday–Friday?
    The Current Lap Swim Hours box says Mon–Thu 7–8pm. The lane-allocation table directly below it says Mon–Friday for the same band. Entered as Mon–Thu.
  • ConflictGuppy and Seal are booked into the same lane at 5:10pm
    Class #85243 (Guppy) and #85260 (Seal) are both recorded on 50m Lane 01, Tuesdays and Thursdays at 5:10. That is the registration system's own data, across all four session dates. Which one is actually in Lane 1?
  • ConflictSaturday 11:00 — lessons or lap swim on Lanes 1–2?
    The allocation table hands lanes 1–14 to lap swim from 11:00 on weekends, while a Saturday Guppy class and two private lessons sit on Lanes 1 and 2. Pool Relay suggests shifting lap swim to lanes 3–16, which is presumably what happens on deck — but the page never says so.
  • OursHow many lanes does Masters actually get?
    The team publishes times but never lanes. We entered 12 — the balance of the 20-lane short course after the city's published public allocation. It closes perfectly in every weekday band, but the Saturday 9:30–10:45 practice has no published allocation to bound it at all.
  • GapIs water exercise running?
    The page lists Tue/Wed/Thu at 8:15 (shallow) and 9:15 (deep), then says "unfortunately we do not have an instructor at this time." Those slots are deliberately not on the calendar — an event still holds water no matter what its description says. Is the water held for them, or released to lap swim?
  • GapWhere do the four teams train?
    Walnut Creek Aquatics is home to the Aquabears (eight practice groups, "practices daily at Clarke"), Masters, the Aquanuts (who hold the 25-meter pool year round for synchro) and the summer Swim Club. Only Masters publishes practice times. The other three occupy real water on a real schedule that nothing public describes.
  • GapWeekend recreational swim — still running, and in which pool?
    The page lists recreational swim under both the 50-meter and the 25-meter pool. We put it in the 25-meter pool, because the allocation table already commits 14 of the 20 short-course lanes to lap swim at that hour.
  • OursWhen do Larkey's weekday sprayground hours run?
    The $3 sprayground-only hours (Mon–Thu, 10:30–12:30 and 4:30–6:30) are published without dates. We used the Memorial Day–Labor Day season that the swim pass names, stopping before Labor Day itself — the center was weekends-only by September.
