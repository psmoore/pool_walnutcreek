Walnut Creek Aquanuts

Synchronized swimming · year round · the 25-meter pool

One of the most acclaimed teams in international synchronized swimming, and home of Olympic champions. A year-round program for swimmers of all ages and abilities. Head coach Kim Probst, (925) 934-4792.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  The 25-meter pool as the public can currently see it.

A pool held year round, and a week that shows two weekend afternoons

The city's own description of Clarke says the 25-meter pool “is used year-round for synchronized swim practice.” The calendar above is everything that pool currently publishes: recreational swim at the weekend, and nothing else.

That gap is not a mistake in the calendar. It is an accurate picture of what Walnut Creek publishes about a pool that one of the most decorated synchro programs in the world trains in every week of the year.

For the Aquanuts the argument is simple: a pool you hold year round should look held. Families planning around practice, the city planning around rentals, and anyone wondering whether the 25-meter pool is free on a Wednesday are all currently guessing.

What is already set up

The Aquanuts exist in Pool Relay as their own organization, and the 25-meter pool exists as its own water at Clarke. Practice times are the only missing piece — the team publishes none, and its website could not be read at all when this page was built.
