Larkey Swim Center

Summer only · closed until Memorial Day

A 25×25 meter heated pool with a one-meter diving board, and an accessible all-abilities sprayground with slides and waterfalls. 2771 Buena Vista Ave, Walnut Creek. Front desk (925) 943-5861.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Shown a month at a time. September is the season ending: weekday sprayground hours, then the last weekends, then the Labor Day opening.

The season ends on a date, not on somebody remembering

Larkey closed after Labor Day and its calendar closed with it, because the whole season was entered as dates rather than as a sentence on a web page.

Nobody has to go back in September and edit “open weekends only through 9/7” off the site. Nobody has to retype the summer next May. Recreational swim, the $3 sprayground hours, the picnic rentals and the private sprayground hour are already in place, waiting for the season to come round.

Page the calendar back to July and the whole summer is there exactly as it ran — which also means next season can be edited now, in February, instead of in a rush in May.

The Summer Recreational Swim Pass covers rec-swim hours at both centers, Memorial Day through Labor Day. It does not cover the $3 inflatable fee at Clarke, and it is not valid for lap swim or water exercise.

One thing we had to guess The $3 weekday sprayground hours are published without any dates. We used the Memorial Day–Labor Day season the swim pass names, stopping the Thursday before Labor Day because the center had already gone to weekends only by September. The real weekday season is the aquatics office's to confirm.
