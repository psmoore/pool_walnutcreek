Walnut Creek Masters

Ages 19–90 · thirteen practices a week · Clarke 50-meter pool

A competitive swimming program and a structured fitness program for adults of all abilities. Head coaches Mike Heaney and Steve Stahl, (925) 280-5421.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Thirteen practices a week, every one in the Clarke 50-meter pool.

The team that completes the city's own lane table

Masters is the one Walnut Creek team that publishes its practice times, and putting them beside the city's published lane counts is what makes both make sense. The city says how many lanes are public; the team says when it is training. Together they account for the pool exactly.
